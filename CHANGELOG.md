# Trikks Reskins

## Unreleased

### Changes
- **Clean Action Buttons**: numpad keybinds are shortened on the buttons: `N1`, `N2`, `s-N3` instead of `Num Pad 1`. On by default, with its own checkbox.

## 1.1

### Newly skinned
Each part has its own checkbox in `/trikks` → Skins, and all of them are on by default.

- **Chat**: flat tabs with an accent line under the selected one, and a flat edit box that lights up while you type. The chat background is still your own setting.
- **XP / reputation bars**: no frame art or divider ticks, just a flat panel behind each bar. Blizzard's bar colours stay.
- **World map + quest log**: flat frame and breadcrumb bar. The quest log side loses its parchment and brown frames. The map itself isn't touched.
- **Quest tracker**: the gold header plates become a thin accent line, and the collapse and filter buttons are grey.
- **Professions book**: flat cards instead of the illustrated boxes, flat skill bars, square spell icons and a small flat "x" to unlearn.

### Everywhere
- Collapsible list headers (recipes, skills, reputation, ...) are flat, and their gold − / + icons are grey.
- Checkboxes look like the ones in the Trikks hub.
- Skill and reputation bars lose their brown, bevelled background.
- Scroll bars are flat, with grey arrows.
- Pop-up buttons (Accept, Decline, ...) and other red buttons are flat.
- The old red − / + squares are grey.
